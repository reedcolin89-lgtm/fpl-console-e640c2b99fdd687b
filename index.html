Estimating points for players with no track record — 4 methods compared

Covers players with no last-season scoring rate to draw on, who have played at least 60 minutes this season. Two separate scores are shown for each method and never combined into one number, because they measure different kinds of error with different consequences: average miss size (how far off the points estimate typically is) and ranking accuracy (whether the method puts players in the right order relative to each other, even if the exact numbers are off) — a method can win on one and lose on the other. Grouped by gameweek, not by player: every no-track-record player in one gameweek used the same model run, so one gameweek counts as one data point regardless of how many such players it had — the reliability check below is gated on the number of gameweeks measured, not the number of players.
